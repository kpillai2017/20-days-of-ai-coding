Choose a mechanism, not just a label

4-stroke: intake, compression, power and exhaust over 720°, using head valves. 2-stroke: a crankcase-scavenged, piston-ported spark-ignition example over 360°. Its descending piston compresses the next crankcase charge; uncovered ports allow transfer and exhaust near the bottom.

Idealised timing, constant crank speed, no real gas pressure, temperatures or combustion dynamics. This does not describe all two-stroke engines (especially diesels). Animation pauses initially if you prefer reduced motion.
