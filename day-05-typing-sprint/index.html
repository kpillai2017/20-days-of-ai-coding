What do the numbers mean?

One typing “word” is five characters, including spaces. WPM uses characters currently matching the passage at the same positions. Accuracy is correct ÷ currently entered characters—not a record of every key you pressed.

The timer continues if you switch tabs. Nothing is uploaded or saved; reload or restart discards the attempt. No global keyboard shortcuts: your normal typing keys belong to the text field.
