The application filters the unchanged source at load time to ASCII A–Z words
of length 2–15 and uppercases them. It currently loads 270,652 unique words.
The list combines US, UK and Australian spellings; it is **not** a licensed
Collins/NWL/official Scrabble lexicon. Some entries may be obscure, offensive,
archaic or unfamiliar. No family-safe filtering is promised. No definitions
are included.

The source README describes a collection refined for Letterpress. CC0 is the

that does not replace this separate data licence. No inference of permission

An initially considered ENABLE mirror was removed because its retrieved
provenance did not supply a direct permission statement. It is not bundled.
