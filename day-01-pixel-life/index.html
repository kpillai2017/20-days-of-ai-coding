A world with four rules

No player. Just possibility.

Every cell checks its eight neighbours. All cells change together, once per generation.

A finite world: cells beyond the board are always dead. Patterns do not wrap around.

  1. Too lonelyFewer than 2 neighbours? A living cell dies.
  2. Just right2 or 3 neighbours? A living cell survives.
  3. Too crowdedMore than 3 neighbours? A living cell dies.
  4. A new beginningExactly 3 neighbours? A dead cell comes alive.
