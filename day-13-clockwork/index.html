HOW A CLOCK KEEPS TIME

Energy, release, rhythm.

A weight supplies energy. A train of gears transmits rotation. The escapement releases it in small steps while returning energy to the pendulum. The pendulum's rhythm regulates the mechanism.

Here the dependency is reversed: your system time drives the picture. The gear ratios suggest meshing wheels, but they do not physically drive the hands. This is a didactic visual model, not a working mechanical design.
